========================
REFUND POLICY
========================

• Refund requests are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
• Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
• Digital products are non-refundable.
• Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

========================
RETURN POLICY
========================

• Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
• Returned items must be unused and include all original accessories.
• Products damaged by customer misuse cannot be returned.

========================
EXCHANGE POLICY
========================

• Products can be exchanged within 10 days of delivery.
• Exchange is subject to stock availability.
• Defective or damaged products are eligible for free exchange.

========================
CANCELLATION POLICY
========================

• Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.
• Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled.
• Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded within 5–7 business days.
